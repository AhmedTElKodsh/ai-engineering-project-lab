# Six-repository review and consolidation

Review began 11 October and completed its expanded source review on 12 October 2026. This is an evidence-based component merge, not a wholesale selection of the largest or newest curriculum. The public home is AhmedTElKodsh/ai-engineering-project-lab; its existing identity and runnable baseline are retained.

## Scope and method

The explicit objective is basic-to-intermediate Python, common applied AI engineering including RAG and agents, then learner-written mathematical primitives and a complete small causal language model trained from random weights. The confirmed cadence is ten hours weekly in five two-hour sessions; CPU completion is required and GTX 1050 Ti use optional/unverified.

Three independent lenses reviewed anonymized A–F snapshots: adversarial defects, branch/boundary behavior and educational acceptance. Reviewers received the same objective and no preferred winner, source names, prior discussion or other reports. D–F were added when requested; their reports remained separate. Repository content can reveal historical identity, so this is name-masked review, not a claim of perfect experimental blinding. Main/default-branch snapshots were inspected first; current local practice and open refinements were separately inventoried, with preservation and provenance limits stated.

Native active contracts/code and relevant checks were reviewed; vendor skill count and archive bulk were not treated as quality. Root verification used original source clones for important findings, including V4's actual 71-problem packaging failure, so snapshot preprocessing is not blamed for that result. Detailed findings with source paths, triggers and check commands remain in [independent reports](independent/acceptance-review.md), [expanded acceptance](independent/acceptance-review-extended.md), [adversarial review](independent/blind-review.md), [expanded adversarial review](independent/blind-review-extended.md), [edge coverage](independent/edge-coverage.md), [edge findings](independent/edge-review.json) and [expanded edge findings](independent/edge-review-extended.json). Anonymous paths refer to backed-up source snapshots, not the final project tree.

## One coherent result and triage

There is one active S0–S8 route, one installed project-mentor, one append-only event log and derived state/LEARNING view. The assistant and model lab live in one cumulative workbench. There are no alternate next-lesson engines, frozen competing curricula, cloned live templates or six copies of coaching state in the active tree. The mechanism shelf is incomplete reference practice, not a second required course. Source-specific faults not carried forward are eliminated by omission, not falsely reported as repaired in their originals.

High-impact carried mechanisms were patched: strict output invariants, empty source rejection, proper source-offset semantics, fail-closed state routing, revision binding, cooperating-writer lock, atomic persistence/recovery, assistance-aware credit, fresh-case transfer, delayed retention and scoped corrections. Old transfer/retention cannot reappear after a repair or changed contract; generated explanations cannot grant learner credit. Current unresolved baseline behavior is explicitly deferred to learner stages: unsupported overlap, partial multi-intent and injection-like queries, plus the original paraphrase. Those five strict xfails are measured limits, not hidden passes. Safety-sensitive future tool operations remain simulated and unimplemented until tested at their stage.

Two independent candidate reviews found active-document and false-credit defects; their original findings are preserved in [candidate state review](independent/candidate-state-review.md) and [candidate acceptance](independent/candidate-acceptance.md). They drove scoped contract hashes, new regressions and synchronized ownership/readiness/offline-branch language. A final recheck and exact candidate results are recorded below after validation. Actual tutor behavior, long-term retention, live RAG quality and model training are not certified by structural tests.

Local bundle/metadata backups are recoverable independently of GitHub deletion. Keep those backups outside the active tutor surface.
