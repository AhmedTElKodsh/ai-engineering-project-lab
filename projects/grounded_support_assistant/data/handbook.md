# Acme Support Handbook

## Password reset

Customers start a password reset from the sign-in page. The reset link is sent to the
verified account email and expires after 30 minutes. Support agents and administrators
cannot view or recover an existing password.

## Refund policy

Customers may request a subscription refund within 14 calendar days of the first charge.
Approved refunds return to the original payment method. Usage-based charges are
non-refundable.

## Single sign-on

Single sign-on (SSO) is available on Business and Enterprise plans. Before SSO can be
enabled, an administrator must verify the organization's email domain. The identity provider
metadata must include a sign-in URL and signing certificate.

## Data export

Organization administrators can request a data export from Settings. The export includes
account profiles, project records, and audit events. The download link remains valid for
24 hours.

## Plan changes

Plan upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Plan downgrades take effect at the
next billing renewal. Canceling a subscription prevents the next renewal but does not erase
stored data.
